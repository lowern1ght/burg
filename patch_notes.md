## 🏘️ Once Upon a Town v0.0.10 Stable release
###  Overall gameplay improvements:
- The village will now build roads only when there is no place remaining for the buildings in the construction queue, this changes the whole tempo and development speed of the village. Instead of growing randomly, the village will grow when it is needed
- Improved builder AI to make it able to open the fence gates, it should no longer be stuck into some buildings
- Greatly improved the road placement, allowing villages to expend way more often that previously, the village should never get stuck
- The townhall is now displayed into the building catalog, allowing you to check what it is producing and check the NBT level preview. You can also check what it adds to the village when upgraded into the upgrade panel
- Made the beginning of the village slower, pushing the player to use the trading system to make it evolve quicker. Most of the buildings are producing less at the beginning, but more when you upgrades it
- Made buildings more tied to each other by adding more prerequisites to some structures, making the progress chain smoother and more realistic
### Trading interface:
- The stock interface got reworked to let you buy resources from the village or sell your own goods. You will need to swap the mode Buy/Sell then click on the arrow to send the resources
- The prices for buy/sell is a complete data driven file, meaning you can add every modded or vanilla items inside of it and set up your own prices
### New buildings:
- Over : helps to cook food elements to bring stronger food to your village and craft some basic charcoal linked items
- New road patterns
