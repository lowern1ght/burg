## 🏘️ Once Upon a Town v0.0.11 Enhanced GUI and better roads !
###  GUI improvements:
- Removed a ton of small elements on the building construction catalog to make is more clean and straight forward. You can now see how much buildings you made in your village and the prerequisites for buildings should be more clear
- Improved the trading interface by adding the price of the elements for buy/sell
- Reworked the village summary to make it more versatile, you can now select an information in particular. Also added the village resume, so you can check what the builder made and when the residents and the herds ate
- Reworked the questing system, allowing you to move items inside the interface instead of sending it through the stock
- Improved the era progress widget to make it smaller and more straight forward, harmonizing the era display between solo era and dual era
### Gameplay improvements:
- Made the town center produces less resources but they are now stronger with upgrades, upgrade triggers new recipes. 
- Made all the craft way more balanced, fixing the emerald price with 'packs' instead or per 'units'
- Modified some building production and building prerequisites to make early game smoother
- Made the eras more specific with the building unlocks so each path is providing a specific resource
### New buildings & roads:
- Added the kitchen building for the 'Cooking' orientation from the pastoral orientation
- Added the well for the agricultural orientation to boost by 2% the village production
- Reworked some road patterns to make these more natural and with more details
### Bug fixing and improvements:
- Fixed a few bug with the interface and textures overlap
- Reworked the road system to allow more elements to be placed instead of only dirt path
- Made the quest spawn less often and reduced the frequency of the food delivery quest
