## Mappings

Burg compiles with **official Mojang mappings (Mojmap)** provided by the
NeoForge toolchain — class/method names like `Level`, `CompoundTag`,
`Level#isClientSide` are the official Mojang names. Yarn names
(`World`, `NbtCompound`, `isClient`) are Fabric-side and will not resolve here.
